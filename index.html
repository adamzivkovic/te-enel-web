About us

A prominent turnkey EPC contractor in Serbia

TERMOELEKTRO ENEL AD is a Belgrade-based EPC contractor delivering engineering, design, installation, testing and commissioning of electrical and I&C systems for thermal power plants, oil & gas facilities and industrial process plants.

Originally established in 1948 as a unit of TERMOELEKTRO, the company became an independent legal entity in 1993 and has operated as a joint-stock company since 2012 — a full-scope EPC contractor from concept design through to handover of a fully tested and commissioned facility.
